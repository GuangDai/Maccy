## Findings (verified)

- `Storage.newBackgroundContext()` — exactly one caller: `StorageBackgroundContextTests`. The production ingest actor builds its context inline (`DefaultSerialModelExecutor(modelContext: ModelContext(modelContainer))` in `BackgroundClipboardIngestor.init`).
- `VisibleWindowLoader.fetchWindow` — zero production callers; 6 callers, all in `StorageBackgroundContextTests`. `History.load()` fetches directly (unbounded `FetchDescriptor`), not through `fetchWindow`.
- The old `fetchWindow` doc claimed "Production calls this…" while a later sentence admitted "not yet wired" — a direct contradiction; the production-claim half was false.

## Why test-only (not delete, not wire)

This section records the D0 rationale before D1 supplied the missing evidence;
it is historical, not the current recommendation.

- **Not delete:** the loader + helper are the scaffolding D1 (windowed load) needs; deleting them throws away working, tested infrastructure and would require re-creating it. They cost nothing test-only (compiled into the test surface only).
- **Not wire now:** wiring `fetchWindow` into `load()` *is* D1 — a behavior change (windowed/lazy load faulting) that is the largest single memory lever (the `2026-06-27-memory-floor-and-retention/` track identifies windowed load as the path toward <100 MB). It is high-effort, high-risk, and deserves its own focused wave, not a drive-by inside D0.
- **Test-only + corrected docs** is honest, preserves the scaffolding, and unblocks D1 whenever it is scheduled — with no false "this is the production path" signal to mislead a future reader.

## What this does NOT do

- Does not change any runtime behavior (doc/comment-only).
- Does not decide *when* D1 happens — that is a memory-track prioritization call.
- Does not touch `History.load()` (still the unbounded fetch until D1).
